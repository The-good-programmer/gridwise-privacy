Gridwise privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Gridwise is a puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores

Gridwise saves your game progress on your device only: puzzles you have solved, puzzles in progress, your streak, your times and hint use, and your settings (theme, sound, vibration, timer, reminder). This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

The app also keeps a short log of errors it runs into, on your device only. If you choose Settings → Help → Send feedback, your phone's share options open with a message you can read and edit first. It includes app and device details, a summary of your play and any recent errors, and it is sent only where you choose (for example, your own email app).

What the app collects and shares

Gridwise does not ask for an account, and it does not collect, send or share personal information. It does not show ads, use analytics, or use your advertising ID.

The app includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK for a future version with ads, but this version never starts it. If a future version shows ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Children

Gridwise is not directed at children under 13.
